# Rules for the AI helper (read this first, every time)

## What you are helping with
Remote30 is a free 30-day challenge for Nigerian designers and developers who want international clients. Owner: Olajide Igbalaye. Read PRD.md for the plan and README.md for how things work.

## The most important rules
1. Never make things up. No fake numbers, no fake testimonials, no fake counters, no "Join 1,000+", no made-up results. If a section has no real content, leave it out and tell me.
2. Never promise clients or money. The honest line is: "I can't promise clients."
3. Never rewrite my words unless I ask. If a text needs changing, suggest it and wait.
4. Never make the sign-up list public. Visitors can add their details. Only I can read them.
5. If you are unsure, ask me. Do not guess.

## Voice (for any words you write)
- Simple words, like talking to a friend. Short lines.
- Be honest, including about bad news.
- Plain English, no big words.
- Never use these words or phrases: unlock, elevate, supercharge, transform, journey, empower, game-changer, leverage, seamless, cutting-edge, revolutionary, unleash, "take it to the next level", "in today's fast-paced world", "dive in", "master".
- Do not use long dashes in the copy.

## Look
- Colours: bright blue gradient (#0B4FC4 to #1E8BFF), dark blue panels (#0A3D9E), cyan (#22E1FF), white, and a light surface (#F3F8FF). All colours live in site-content.ts.
- One font: Nunito. Headlines heavy (900).
- Signature: "Remote" in white and "30" in cyan, a tilted dark blue panel for the main promise, short cyan slash marks, soft corner circles.
- Not allowed: stock photos, emojis, icon grids, rocket or lightbulb icons, three cards in a row, fake dashboards, the LinkedIn logo, glossy 3D shapes.
- Buttons are big and easy to tap. Design for a small Android phone first. Keep the page light and fast.

## How to work
- Work in small steps. Show me what you changed in plain words after each step.
- Keep every section as its own piece, in the order in PRD.md.
- All text and settings live in site-content.ts. Do not scatter words through the code.
- Save a copy of the current version before big changes. Work on a separate branch. Never go live without my OK.
- Secret keys never go in the code that visitors can see. They live in the hosting settings.
- Update README.md and PRD.md when something changes, in plain words.

## Before you say "done"
- Check the page at phone size and desktop size.
- Check every text is easy to read on its background.
- Check the sign-up form works, and the group link works.
- Search for the banned words above and remove them.
- List what still needs my input.
