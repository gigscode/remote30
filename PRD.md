# Remote30: Product Plan (PRD)

Written in plain words. Update it whenever the plan changes.

## What this is
Remote30 is a free 30-day challenge, run in public by Olajide Igbalaye. The website has one job at first: get the right people to join the WhatsApp group where the challenge happens.

## Who it is for
Nigerian designers and developers who want international clients. Most will open the site on a phone, on mobile data.

## The problem
They apply to many jobs and hear nothing. They have no clear message, no plan, and no rooms where people who hire spend time. Olajide lived this himself for years.

## The promise (keep it honest)
30 days to a clearer profile, one portfolio page that sells, and real conversations with people who hire abroad. We do NOT promise clients. We do NOT promise a number of clients.

## What "done" looks like for a member at Day 30
- A profile a stranger understands in 5 seconds
- One portfolio page (who you help, what they got, what to do next)
- 100 helpful comments
- 15 real conversations with people who could hire

## How we know it is working
Numbers to watch, in this order:
1. Visitors who become sign-ups
2. Sign-ups who actually join the WhatsApp group
3. Members who do the weekly actions
4. Members who finish the 30 days
5. Members who get a real reply, a call, or a client
Do not celebrate likes or page visits alone.

## Version 1: Sign-up page (now)
Includes: the hero, my story, the four weeks, the finish line, the honest box, who it is for, the sign-up form, the thank-you screen with the group link, questions, footer.
Does NOT include: results, testimonials, prices, resources, payments, logins.

## Version 2: Results and offers (after 30 days, only with real content)
Adds: real results, real testimonials (with permission), real offers, free resources, the next start date and waiting list, and a private page for Olajide to see the sign-up list.
Rule: if there is no real content for a section, that section does not appear.

## Voice
Simple words. Short lines. Like a person talking. Honest about the bad parts. No hype. No big promises.

## Look
Bright blue and cyan, white text, a heavy rounded font, the "30" in cyan, a tilted dark blue panel for the main promise. No stock photos. No icon grids. No 3D LinkedIn logo.

## Privacy
The sign-up list is private. It is used only to run Remote30. It is never sold or shown on the site.

## Open questions
- What exactly is in the paid done-for-you package, and how many days does it take?
- How many people can Olajide support well at the same time?
- When do we start taking payments on the site, and how?
- When does the group move beyond WhatsApp, if ever?
